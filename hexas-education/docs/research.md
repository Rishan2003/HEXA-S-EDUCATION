## Suggested timeline

2007: Educational journey begins, according to institutional histories. 2017: HEXA’S appears in British Council registration-centre brochure. 2018: Expanded historical location listings; institutional site reports Top Performer recognition. 2026: supplied award photographs document Highest Volume Contributor Bangladesh and Highest Growth South Asia recognition. Do not invent annual milestones to fill gaps.

## Missing information requiring HEXA’S approval

1. Signed current branch register, active status, manager/contact, campus vs test venue address, map pin and courses for every branch. Four locations above are a verified subset, not the entire network.
2. Exact five consecutive award years, award certificates/issuer announcement URLs and named recipients. The user’s five-year statement is recorded, not silently discarded; public evidence currently supports five recognitions without an auditable year sequence.
3. Bangladesh legal entity name, registered address, current leadership titles and approved biographies. Zindabazar pages inconsistently state 2015, 2016, 2018 and “19 years”; do not use any as branch founding date without clarification.
4. Exact 2026 ceremony dates, location, issuer wording and media reproduction permissions. Supplied images may be used for this commissioned website but public redistribution rights should be confirmed.
5. Student consent and original result verification before publishing personal testimonials or score documents. Do not infer general success rates from individual results.
6. Current institutional mission and vision approval; original copy may communicate learning, English skills and opportunity without inventing formal policy language.
7. Junior Spoken English branch availability is not verified. Basic Computer and Graphic Design are verified at Ambarkhana, not across the network.
8. Privacy controller, inquiry retention period, admissions recipient email, working WhatsApp authorization for each branch and production domain.

## Access limitations and exclusions

The supplied Facebook share links [one](https://www.facebook.com/share/1EHrVNy8Re/) and [two](https://www.facebook.com/share/1DzrvVvwn5/) could not be fetched. `https://www.hexasmajortila.com/` was inaccessible through the research browser. `https://education.hexasusa.com/about-us/` returned 404. Public social mirrors were treated only as leads, not authoritative substitutes. No British Council-origin announcement confirming the five exact annual awards was found. No invented data fills those gaps.

Exclude unsupported “#1 institution”, visa success percentages, nationwide record-holder claims, invented learners served, teacher certifications, guaranteed IELTS scores and generic university partner logos. Historical British Council relationship and current official IELTS directory listings do not grant universal partnership status to every branch.
